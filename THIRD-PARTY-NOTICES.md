the author's rights. An open-source licence has not been selected.

DM Sans and Manrope retain SIL Open Font License notices in
`apps/web/public/fonts/DM-Sans-OFL.txt` and `Manrope-OFL.txt`. Keep them with font

need separate rights/contracts. Source rights do not authorize voice assets or
client material. Visual media needs provenance/permitted-use review before
redistribution; publication makes no third-party clearance claim. See
[legal/privacy](docs/legal-and-privacy.md).
